# Archive

- 24 July 2019, Montreal, Canada, Interactive tutorial on making the most of daylong recordings at [CogSci 2019](https://cognitivesciencesociety.org/cogsci-2019/) by John Bunce, Elika Bergelson, Anne Warlaumont, and Marisa Casillas. All materials available [here](https://github.com/aclew/DaylongDataTutorial-CogSci19).
